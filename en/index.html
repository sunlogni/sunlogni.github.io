Step 1: split by budget & scale (selection buckets)

Decide first whether you are personal-free, personal-paid, enterprise, or self-host, then pick a product within that bucket.

Personal, free is enoughSunlogin Free · ToDesk Free · Chrome Remote Desktop · Quick Assist
Home / helping family / multi-device: start with a free tier. Sunlogin Free is cross-platform with no time/session cap; for one-off fixes use the built-in Quick Assist.
Personal, pay for qualitySunlogin Guazi/Super · ToDesk Pro
Want 2K/4K, 144fps, unthrottled transfer, remote CMD: the Chinese paid tiers are great value — Sunlogin from CNY 158/yr, ToDesk from CNY 158/yr.
Enterprise & complianceSunlogin Enterprise · ToDesk Enterprise · TeamViewer · AnyDesk
Need central management, session audit/recording, SSO/AD, silent deploy: pick an Enterprise plan; for cross-border teams look at TeamViewer/AnyDesk (mind annual billing and currency).
Data sovereignty / self-hostRustDesk · Microsoft RDP
Keep data inside your network: RustDesk self-hosting; pure intranet with a Win Pro license: just use built-in RDP at zero extra cost.

Quick scenarios

  • Just occasionally helping your parents install software: Sunlogin Free or Windows Quick Assist — zero cost, no need to buy VIP.
  • Frequent remote work needing crisp video/design: Sunlogin Super or ToDesk Pro for 4K/144fps and unthrottled transfer.
  • Managing hundreds of devices with audit: go Enterprise (Sunlogin Enterprise / ToDesk Standard) for SSO, session recording, silent deploy.
  • Connecting to overseas machines: TeamViewer / AnyDesk overseas nodes are steadier, but remember annual billing in USD/EUR.
  • Data must never leave the intranet: self-host RustDesk, or use Microsoft RDP on a pure LAN (host needs Win Pro).
  • TeamViewer free flagged you as commercial and cut the session: switch to a Chinese free tier (Sunlogin/ToDesk) or buy a license.
